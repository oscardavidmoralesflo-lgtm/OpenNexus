How it works

From zero to your first agent in an afternoon

No coding needed. If you know how your client serves its customers, you have what it takes.

  1. Create the client and its agent

    Pick the industry, the tone and the AI model. Start from a template and adjust it in minutes.

  2. Teach it the business

    Upload PDFs, FAQs and prices. The agent answers from that information instead of making things up.

  3. Connect channels and hand over the portal

    WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, web or phone. Your client signs in to a portal with its own brand.

Security

Agentaria is designed for agencies that handle other businesses' information.

  • Roles and two-step verificationOwner, admin, operator and viewer. Require 2FA for your whole team.

FAQ

What people usually ask

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You create agents with a wizard, upload documents and connect channels from the dashboard.

How is AI usage billed?

Use your own keys for providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or OpenRouter, or prepaid credit. Reports show the cost per client so you can bill it on.

Does it work with official WhatsApp?

Yes. We recommend Meta's official Cloud API for each number. You can also link a number by QR code for testing.

Do my clients see Agentaria?

They don't have to. Their portal shows their logo and colors and can run on their own domain.

What if the AI can't answer?

It escalates the conversation to a person on your team under the rules you set, and sends a notification.

What happens when the trial ends?

Pick a plan to continue. Otherwise your account is paused and your data is kept for 30 days.
